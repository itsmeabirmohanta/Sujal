## The root causes

**1. CMD_CALIBRATE "doesn't listen"** — `CAL_STILL_DPS` was `3.0` dps, but [Config.h](vritra_fc/Config.h) itself records the *measured* gyro noise floor on this part as **4.3–11.2 dps pp**. The stillness gate compares peak-to-peak spread against that threshold, so on a perfectly motionless bench it could **never** be satisfied. It re-armed forever, hit the 20 s timeout, dropped back to idle keeping the old bias — and printed nothing. The command was received and executed every time; it just could not physically converge. Now `15.0` dps, and every exit path reports.

Separately, the 3 s settle delay was being applied to *commanded* re-zeros. That delay exists so a noisy first sample at boot isn't frozen in as "0 forever" — it has no meaning when you've deliberately placed the vehicle and the filter has been converged for minutes. Commanded datum is now captured on the next IMU sample. **I did not touch the atan2 formula, the 90° axis swap, or `ATT_SIGN_*`.**

**2. Barometer** — there were **two** pad-reference variables that were never connected: `padPresPa` (persisted to EEPROM) and a file-scope `groundPressurePa` (what `getAltitudeAGL` actually read). `restoreCalibration()` wrote only the first, so **every reboot silently reverted the altitude datum to 101325 Pa** — at your 97.3 kPa pad that's ~340 m of error, and it's the direct cause of `ALTITUDE AGL −10.40 m` and `BARO VS GNSS ΔALT 263.70 m` in your screenshot. Also fixed: the 320 ms blocking `delay()` loop, averaging *raw* reads instead of median-filtered ones (one torn burst ≈ 1024 Pa ≈ 90 m, baking ~5.6 m of permanent offset), 20 ms sampling against a 40 ms sensor ODR, and the fixed `44330.8` constant — that's ISA-15 °C, and at your 28.9 °C the true scale height is 46469, so **every altitude read 4.8 % low**: ~48 m at 1000 m apogee, ~29 m at the 600 m deployment trigger that has a ±10 m tolerance.

**6. NavIC** — `$PMTK353,1,1,1,0,1` was commented "GPS + GLONASS + Galileo + NavIC". MediaTek's actual parameter order is `<GPS>,<GLONASS>,<GALILEO>,<GALILEO_FULL>,<BEIDOU>`. Now sent, with `$PAIR001` ACKs parsed and reported. Also fixed the GSV cycle commit, which was anchored on `$GPGSV` being present — making GPS a prerequisite for reporting NavIC.

**7. LoRa 2 km** — the big one. `setTxPower()` early-returned on `dbm == txPower_` but only wrote the chip when idle. At the BOOT→LAUNCH transition the radio is in `RS_TXING` ~19 times out of 20 (944 ms of a 1000 ms cycle), so `txPower_` became 14 while the chip stayed at 10 — and every later call hit the early-out. **The vehicle flew the whole mission at +10 dBm believing it was at +14.** That's 4 dB, ~37 % of your range.
