## The real problem was never capacity

**Not one of your 12 footprints has an `F.Courtyard`** — and neither do the 47 footprint instances inside `Board1.kicad_pcb`. When a footprint has no courtyard, KiCad falls back to the bounding box of whatever geometry exists. For most of yours that's **the pad row only, 1.7 mm tall**:

| Footprint | What DRC was checking | Real module body |
|---|---|---|
| BMP585_hdr8 | 19.5 × **1.7** mm | 20.5 × 15.5 mm |
| MAX98357A_hdr7 | 16.9 × **1.7** mm | 19.4 × 17.8 mm |
| W25Q64_hdr6 | 14.4 × **1.7** mm | 22.0 × 18.0 mm |
| DS3231_hdr6 | 14.4 × **1.7** mm | 23.0 × 38.5 mm |

So DRC was inventing collisions between pad-row boxes *and* hiding real ones where two bodies overlapped by 14 mm with no pads in common. The earlier "proof" that Board 1 can't fit its parts came from that bad data. Every hour of nudging was chasing a measurement error.
