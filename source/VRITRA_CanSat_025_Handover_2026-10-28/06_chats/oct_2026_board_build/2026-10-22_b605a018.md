## 3. Hardware traps for the zero board
- **INA226:** if your module's shunt resistor is 0.1 Ω, it can only measure up to 0.82 A. Use it for bus voltage only (telemetry field 7), and never run servo or ESC current through it.
- **microSD module:** the common blue module doesn't release its MISO line, so it must be the only device on its SPI bus. That's why it goes alone on the Teensy 4.0. The two 4.1s use their built-in card slots.
- **DS3231 (ZS-042):** this module tries to charge its backup cell. Remove the charging resistor if you use a CR2032.
- **ICM-20948:** connect CS and SDO to the module's 1V8 pin, never to 3.3 V. Set the address to 0x69 so it doesn't clash with the DS3231 at 0x68.
- **XIAO Sense:** don't use D8, D9 or D10 (the camera's SD card uses them). Leave D2 free, because it's a boot strapping pin.
- **Teensy:**
  - Nothing connects to the On/Off or Program pins.
  - Cut the VIN–VUSB pad if USB and external 5 V will be connected together.
  - None of the pins tolerate 5 V, so pull the Hall sensor output up to 3.3 V.
- **Signals between boards:** put a 1 kΩ resistor in series at the driving end of every one, so a powered board can't feed current into an unpowered Teensy.
- **A20CLM servo:** at 5 V it gives only 6.8 kg·cm (10.2 needs 8.4 V), and AGFRC publishes no stall current, so you'll need to measure it. Your CDR says A20BHM, but the part you own is the A20CLM.
- **Missing from your list but required by the rules:** a beacon of at least 92 dB, a Hall sensor for the gyro spin rate (field 14), an external switch with LED, and a BMS.

- **A thin line along the holes** is a solder trail or bare tinned wire on the bottom of the board. It's drawn as if you could see through the board, so left and right match the parts on top and you don't have to mirror anything.
- **A small arch** is a short insulated wire jumping over another line. Don't put solder in the hole under the arch.

- **The legend** on the right gives each colour in plain words, such as "GPS TX → Teensy 7", "LoRa CS (29)" or "heartbeat → Backup plug".
