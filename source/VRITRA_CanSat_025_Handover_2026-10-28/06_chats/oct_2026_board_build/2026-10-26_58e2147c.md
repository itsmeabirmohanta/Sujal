## 3. INA260 wiring (you have one)
Put it in the **battery position (U1)** on the POWER board, in series with the main battery line:
- Battery **+** → rocker switch → fuse → INA260 **IN+**.
- INA260 **IN−** → the VBAT bus, which feeds the ESC and the buck converters.
- **VCC** → 3.3 V, **GND** → GND, **SDA/SCL** → POWER J2 → Board 1 J2 → Teensy pins 18/19.
- Leave the address pins at GND (address 0x40). Address 0x47 would clash with the BMP585.
- Bridge the two empty INA positions (U2, U3) with plain wire from input to output.

**Charging status:** a positive current means the pack is discharging. A negative current only appears if the charger connects on the IN− side. The INA260 can't see a charger clipped straight onto the battery leads.

**Before wiring:** I couldn't find a 7Semi pinout for this board. Check on yours whether its VBUS pin is tied to IN+ or IN−.

## 4. INA280

- It doesn't measure voltage or power itself.

The INA260 you already have is the better fit here.

## INA260 pin connections (POWER board, "INA260 #1 BATTERY" spot)
Pins top to bottom on the module's 8-pin header. Check them against the labels printed on your module first.

| Pin | Name | Connects to | Wire |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | VCC | 3.3 V from buck **M4** pin 3 | thin jumper OK |
| 2 | GND | board GND | thin jumper OK |
| 3 | SCL | POWER **J2 pin 1** → cable → MAIN J2 pin 1 → Teensy pin 19 | thin jumper OK |
| 4 | SDA | POWER **J2 pin 2** → cable → MAIN J2 pin 2 → Teensy pin 18 | thin jumper OK |
| 5 | ALERT | nothing | leave empty |
| 6 | VBUS | the **VBAT bus** (same wire as IN−) | thin jumper OK (it only measures voltage) |
| 7 | **IN+** | battery + coming **from the fuse** | **THICK wire** |
| 8 | **IN−** | the **VBAT bus**: ESC (POWER J4), buck inputs M1/M2/M3, the 470 µF capacitor, POWER J10 | **THICK wire** |

Leave the A0/A1 address pads alone, so the chip answers at 0x40.

## How the battery goes through it
Only the **+ wire** goes through the INA260. Battery − goes **straight to GND** and never touches IN+ or IN−.

```
Battery + (XT60, POWER J1 pin 1)
   -> rocker switch (POWER J3: pin 1 in, pin 2 out)
   -> fuse F1 10 A (or a thick wire link for now)
   -> INA260 IN+  (pin 7)
        [current flows through the 2 mΩ resistor inside the INA260]
   -> INA260 IN-  (pin 8)
   -> VBAT bus -> ESC, 5 V buck, 5 V servo buck, 8 V buck, voltage sense

Battery − (POWER J1 pin 2) -> GND (all boards share this GND)
```

**Current direction:** battery side on IN+, load side on IN−. Then a positive current means "discharging". If you swap the two, nothing breaks, but the current reads negative.

## ⚠ Do NOT use jumper wires for IN+, IN− and the battery path
Normal jumper wires are about 24–28 AWG and safe for only about **1–2 A**. Your battery path also feeds the ESC and the gyro-wheel motor, which can pull **10–20 A or more** when it spins up. A thin jumper there will get hot, melt or burn. Use thin jumpers only for pins 1–6.

For the high-current path (battery → switch → fuse → IN+ → IN− → VBAT bus → ESC):
- **Wire:** use **18 AWG silicone wire, or thicker** (16 AWG is better for the motor).
- **Header pins:** a 2.54 mm header pin is only good for about 3 A. If your 7Semi INA260 has a **screw terminal or big solder pads** for IN+/IN− (often labelled VIN+ / VIN−), put the thick wires **there, directly on the module**, not through the board's header holes.
- **Limit:** the INA260 is rated **15 A** continuous, so the 10 A fuse also protects it.

No, careful. **IN− is NOT ground.** You're right that IN− and VBUS are joined together, but that point is **+12 V**, not ground.

Battery +  ->  switch  ->  (fuse/link)  ->  IN+  [INA260]  IN-  ->  +12 V bus (VBAT) -> ESC, bucks

                                                             VBUS (joined to IN-, also +12 V)

Battery −  ------------------------------------------------>  GND (INA260 GND pin 2, bucks GND, ESC GND, all boards)

| INA260 pin | Voltage on it | Connects to |
|---|---|---|
| 7 IN+ | +12 V (battery side) | battery + after the switch/fuse |
| 8 IN− | +12 V (load side) | VBAT bus → ESC +, buck inputs |
| 6 VBUS | +12 V | joined to IN− (only measures voltage) |
| 2 GND | 0 V | battery −, common ground |
| 1 VCC | 3.3 V | M4 3.3 V buck output |
