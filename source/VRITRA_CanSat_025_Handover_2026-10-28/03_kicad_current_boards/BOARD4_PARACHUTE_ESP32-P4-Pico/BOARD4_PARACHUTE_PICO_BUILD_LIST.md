# VRITRA Board 4 PARACHUTE — ESP32-P4-Pico zero board — BUILD LIST

**Hole numbers:** C = column counted from the LEFT edge of the board (C1 = the first hole column, beside the LSM6DSO32). R = row counted DOWN from the TOP (R1 = the top hole row = the row of P4 pin 1 / GPIO54).
TRAIL = solder trail / bare tinned wire on the SOLDER side through every hole of the run, 90° turns only.
LINK = insulated wire on the COMPONENT side between the two holes (it jumps over other trails).

## 0. Before you start
- The ESP32-P4-Pico STAYS where it is: left pins in **C13 R1..R20** (pin 1 GPIO54 = C13 R1, the top row), right pins in **C20 R1..R20** (pin 40 VBUS = C20 R1). Count every other hole from these.
- REMOVE the LSM6DSO32, the BMP585 and all temporary header pins / Dupont wires (grey, black, yellow, green).
- Refit the **BMP585** with its 8 pins in **C6 R13..R20**: VIN at the TOP (C6R13), INT at the bottom (C6R20), its board lying to the RIGHT (toward the P4). Chip side up.
- Refit the **LSM6DSO32** with its 9-pin row in **R23 C8..C16**: INT2 at C8, VIN at C16, its board hanging DOWN (toward R29). Chip side up. Do NOT fit the 5-pin back row.
- Both are rotations of how they sat before (no flipping): labels stay readable from above.

## 4. Test before plugging anything in
1. Meter on beep, NO power: +5V_LOGIC, +3V3_B, +8V_STEER, +5V_SERVO each to GND → must NOT beep. +8V_STEER to +5V_SERVO → must NOT beep.
2. Each signal trail to its neighbours → must NOT beep.
3. POWER board only (no P4 / servos): J8 shows 5 V and 3.3 V on the right pins.
4. Then plug the P4-Pico power, then servos one at a time.
